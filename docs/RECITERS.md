# Reciter catalogue

The Al-Bayan reader loads the available reciter catalog dynamically from the MP3Quran API. The **Find reciter** field filters the complete list returned by that provider; it is not restricted to a small hard-coded shortlist.

## Availability and rights

The list above is a requested priority catalogue, not a claim that every provider currently supplies every reciter, every Surah, or verse-level audio. The application should only expose a reciter when a provider returns a valid recording and the recording's identity and reuse terms have been checked. Verse-level audio and timing may be supplied by a different provider from full-Surah recordings. Do not substitute another reciter's audio when a requested recording is unavailable.

Before public release, verify reciter names, reading narration (riwayah), audio coverage, timing data, attribution, and redistribution permissions.
